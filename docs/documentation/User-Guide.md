# User Guide

## Teacher Flow

Teachers use ClassSaga to manage classrooms and award points to students as positive reinforcement for desired behaviors.

### Getting Started
1. Register for an account and select the **Teacher** role
2. Log in to access the Teacher Dashboard

### Creating a Classroom
1. From the Teacher Dashboard, click **Create Classroom**
2. Enter a classroom name and any optional details
3. Once created, your classroom will appear on the dashboard with a unique join code
4. Share the join code with students so they can enroll

### Awarding Points
1. From the Teacher Dashboard, select a classroom
2. Scroll down to navigate to the Class Roster
3. To award points to an individual student, click on the student and enter a point amount and reason and click Award Points
4. To award points to multiple students the same amount of points for the same reason, just click on all of the students that should get the points, enter the point amount and reason, and click Award Points. This can be done for the entire class by clicking the Select All button which will select all students
5. Students will see their updated point total immediately

### Viewing Student Progress
1. Select a classroom from the Teacher Dashboard
2. Click the Student Overview button to view their current points, active pet stats including level, hunger, happiness, and cleanliness, and last active date

## Student Flow

Students use ClassSaga to care for and customize their virtual pet using points earned from their teacher.

### Getting Started
1. Register for an account and select the **Student** role
2. Log in. You will be prompted to choose a starter pet if you do not have one yet
3. Choose one of the available starter pets (and give it a name) to begin

### Joining a Classroom
1. From the Student Dashboard, click **Join Classroom**
2. Enter the join code provided by your teacher
3. The classroom will appear on your dashboard once joined

### Caring for Your Pet
1. Navigate to **My Pet** from the Student Dashboard
2. Each day you can perform one free **Feed**, **Play**, and **Brush** action
3. Additional care actions can be performed for 10 points each
4. Care actions increase your pet's care stats and earn experience points toward leveling up

### Spending Points
1. Navigate to the **Shop** from the Student Dashboard or navbar to browse available items
2. Purchase special care items to use on your pet, cosmetic backgrounds and accessories to customize the pet scene, or additional pets
3. Purchased items appear in your inventory on the **My Pet** page. Just switch the tab from Care to **Inventory** or **Pets**

### Customizing Your Pet
1. On the **My Pet** page, switch to the **Inventory** tab
2. Use a care item by going to the item and clicking **Use** (care items are a one time use item)
3. Equip a background or accessory by selecting it and clicking **Equip**
4. Click on an equipped item slot to unequip it
5. To switch between owned pets, go to the **Pets** tab and click **Switch**
6. Pets can be renamed by clicking the pencil icon next to their name when they are active

### Battling Monsters
1. Navigate to **Battle** from the Student Dashboard or navbar
2. Each day you get one free battle attempt against the current monster
3. Click **Attack** to start the battle. it plays out turn by turn automatically
4. Your pet's strength, speed, and defense stats determine battle performance
5. Monster HP persists between days. It may take multiple attempts to defeat a monster
6. Defeating a monster rewards your pet with XP and drops a random item into your inventory
7. Each defeated monster is replaced by a stronger one to match your pet's growing stats
